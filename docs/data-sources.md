# Data sources

The project uses publicly available historical energy datasets. Check the source pages for current definitions, coverage, and licensing/attribution requirements before redistributing downloaded files.

| Dataset | Source | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Oil production | [Our World in Data – oil production](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/oil-production-by-region) | Annual country-level production |
| Oil consumption | [Our World in Data – oil consumption](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/oil-consumption-by-region-terawatt-hours-twh) | Annual country-level consumption |
| Crude oil prices | [Our World in Data – crude oil prices](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/crude-oil-prices) | Annual crude oil price trend |
| Country/region mapping | [Our World in Data – world regions](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/world-regions-according-to-the-energy-institute) | Map countries to regions |
| Imports and exports | [U.S. Energy Information Administration – International](https://www.eia.gov/international/data/) | Country-level oil trade series |

The analysis period is 2000–2018 and the selected countries are Brazil, Canada, China, India, Iran, Iraq, Mexico, Norway, Russia, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and the United States.

## Units and conversion

- Production and consumption: TWh.
- Imports and exports: thousand barrels per day (kb/d), annual average rates.
- Crude oil prices: if the downloaded source values are in USD per cubic metre, convert to USD per barrel by dividing by 6.2898. Confirm the source unit before applying this conversion. The resulting values are current USD unless separately adjusted for inflation.
